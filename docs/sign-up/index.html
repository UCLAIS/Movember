How to sign up

Create a team on JustGiving or Movember, then send us your links. We’ll add your society to the leaderboard.

Create your society’s team

Use JustGiving for your chosen charity, or Movember to fundraise for men’s health. Already have a team? You can send your links.

JustGiving

A team page brings your members’ fundraising together in one place.

Every member can set up their own page. They can share it and collect donations themselves, with the money counting towards your society’s team total.

  1. Sign in to JustGiving and create a fundraising page for your chosen charity.
  2. Open your fundraising page and select “Create a team”.
  3. Add your society’s name, a short story and a team target. Add a photo, review the details and publish your team page.
  4. Share the team link with your members. Each person creates their own fundraising page and connects it to the team.

More detail: JustGiving’s team setup guide.

Movember

Use a Movember team to bring your society’s fundraising for men’s health together.

  1. Sign up to Movember, or log in, and set up your personal Mo Space.
  2. Open the team section of your Mo Space and create a new team. The person who creates it becomes the team captain.
  3. Use your society’s name, set a fundraising target and add your motivation and a team photo.
  4. Share your public team page with your members and invite them to join through their own Movember accounts.

More detail: Movember’s team setup guide (PDF, page 11).

Send us your links

Once your team page is live, email president@uclai.co.uk with:

  • A link to your society’s page.
  • A link to your JustGiving or Movember team page.

We’ll use these to add your society to the leaderboard.
